# Contributing

This repository is a personal lab: it describes one specific cluster on one
specific host, and it changes only in step with that cluster. Pull requests
from outside are therefore not accepted.

What is welcome:

- **Questions.** If something in the guides is unclear or does not work when
  you repeat it, open an issue and say which guide and which step.
- **Corrections.** A wrong command, a broken link or an outdated statement in
  the documentation: open an issue describing it.
- **Ideas.** If you know a better way to do something, open an issue with the
  reasoning. Settled designs are recorded in `docs/decisions.md`; an issue
  that brings new evidence against one of them is the most useful kind.

Please do not post secrets, tokens or addresses of your own infrastructure in
an issue.

You are free to copy anything here into your own lab under the terms of the
[MIT license](LICENSE).
